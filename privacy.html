Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

WHATIF is a roleplay and entertainment app for adults aged 19 and over. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. We collect only what we need to run the app.

What we collect

  • Account details: a username and password, or your Google account email if you sign in with Google.
  • Profile (persona): persona name, birth year, gender, occupation, bio and the avatar photos you choose to add. Personas may not reflect your real identity.
  • Phone number: stored privately, not verified by us and never shown to other members. It is used to limit the number of accounts per number and for support.
  • Approximate location: your area and city only, never your exact position. Location metadata is removed from photos you upload.
  • Selfie check: a selfie is taken to confirm a real person is using the app. A human-face check runs on your device; the selfie is stored privately, is never shown to other members and is deleted within about 30 days. It is not an ID check.
  • Content you create: chat messages, photos, voice notes, channel posts, friend requests, likes and matches.
  • Safety data: reports and blocks you make or receive.
  • Device data: a device identifier, push notification token and basic error logs so notifications and the app work reliably.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Camera: selfie check and photos you send.
  • Microphone: recording voice notes you send.
  • Approximate location: showing your area and city.
  • Photos: choosing pictures for your persona and chats.
  • Notifications: alerts for messages, matches and requests.

How we use it

  • To create your account, show your persona to other adult members, match you and deliver messages.
  • To keep the community safe: our moderation team can review chats, images and channel content, especially when reported. Messages are secured in transit; we do not claim they are end-to-end encrypted.
  • To prevent abuse, enforce our Terms and Community Standards, and meet legal duties.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal data and we do not show third-party ads. We use service providers that process data for us: Supabase (database, storage and sign-in, hosted in India), Google (Google Sign-in and Firebase Cloud Messaging for notifications) and Google Play (distribution and payments). We may disclose information when the law requires it or to protect people from harm, including reports about minors.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is active. Selfies are deleted within about 30 days. When you delete your account, your persona, matches, chats and photos are removed. Reported content may be retained for the period the law requires and is then deleted automatically.

Your choices and rights

You can edit your persona, block and report people, and delete your account in the app (Profile → ⋮ → Delete account) or on our account deletion page. Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 you may ask us to access, correct or erase your data by emailing us.

Children

WHATIF is for adults only (19+). We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 19. See our Child Safety Standards.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit and access to it is restricted. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for major changes, tell you in the app.

Grievance contact

Email whatifnow369@gmail.com. Complaints are acknowledged and resolved within the timelines set by India's IT Rules 2021.
