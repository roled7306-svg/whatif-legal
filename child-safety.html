Child Safety Standards

Last updated: 18 October 2026

WHATIF is for adults only. Everyone must be 19 or older by birth year and complete a selfie check.

  • Reports about minors go to the top of the moderation queue and the account is removed as soon as the report is reviewed. A separate record is kept for legal follow-up.
  • Text and channel content are checked for child-related content and escalated to the top of the moderation queue. Photos are reviewed when they are reported.
  • To report child safety concerns email whatifnow369@gmail.com or use the Report button in the app.
